About NexaLearn

NexaLearn — Learn. Understand. Master.

An independent AI-powered learning platform. Structured lessons, personalised explanations, interactive practice.

AI modes

  • Live — replies from Groq via NexaLearn's secure backend.
  • Simulated — built-in offline tutor when the backend is unreachable.

Privacy

Your progress is stored only in this browser's local storage. The Groq API key lives on the server, never in your browser.

Offline use

NexaLearn is installable as a Progressive Web App. After the first visit, you can open it from your home screen and study lessons even without internet. The AI tutor falls back to Simulated mode while offline.
